MediTrack

Privacy policy & support

Updated 6 October 2026 · Version 1.1.0 onward

MediTrack helps you organize medicine reminders, dose history, profiles, and stock counts. Your medicine records stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Contact the developer at p4to.dev@gmail.com.

Records stored on your device

The app stores the profile names, medicine names, dosages, schedules, stock counts, dose history, settings, and optional medicine photos that you enter. These records are used to display your lists, schedule reminders, and update history and stock. MediTrack has no account login or developer-operated cloud database. The app does not send these health records to the developer or use them to target advertisements. Android app backup is disabled in this version.

Advertising and privacy choices

MediTrack is free to use and uses Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers including the Android advertising ID when available, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. These support advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. The app uses Google's consent flow where required and checks whether ads may be requested before loading ads. You can revisit available choices in Settings → Advertising privacy choices.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Free features

All app features, including adding medicines, creating family profiles, and exporting CSV records, are free. MediTrack has no subscriptions or in-app purchases and does not collect payment details.

Permissions and reminders

Notifications, alarm access, vibration, wake locks, full-screen alerts, and foreground audio are used to deliver reminders. Restart and clock-change events restore schedules after the device has been unlocked. Photo selection lets you attach a medicine picture; a local copy is stored by the app. Internet access supports ads and consent. Android settings control the permissions you grant. Denying permissions, force-stopping the app, or device power restrictions may delay or prevent reminders.

Exports, support, and disclosure

If you export or share a CSV file, it can contain profile and medicine details and dose history. You choose its destination; that destination's own privacy practices apply. If you email support, we receive the address, message, and attachments you send and use them to handle your request. Avoid sending unnecessary health information. Support messages may remain in email records until deleted; you may request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to any records we are required to retain.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until deleted or app storage is cleared. Archiving a medicine stops its reminders but preserves history. To erase all local profiles, medicines, photos, history, and settings, use Android Settings → Apps → MediTrack → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This cannot be undone. Exported or shared copies must be deleted separately. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase records stored only on your phone. Google's advertising records follow Google's retention and deletion policies.

A reminder tool

MediTrack is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions. Follow your prescription and consult a healthcare professional for medical advice. Do not rely on this app as your only reminder or use it for emergency care.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions, deletion requests concerning support messages, or app support, email p4to.dev@gmail.com.
